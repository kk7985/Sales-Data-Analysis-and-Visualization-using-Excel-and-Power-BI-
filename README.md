1) Summary
This report analyses E-Commerce Data from 2023-2025 across different regions. The Objective is to
analyses E-commerce data and also highlight why 2025 get lower trends compare to first half month
of 2023 and 2024, This analysis identifies top-performing categories, peak sales periods and areas
where improvements can increase overall profitability.

2) Objective
 The main objectives of this analysis are
 Analyse overall revenue performance.
 Identify top selling products and categories.
 Understand customer Purchasing behaviour based on return products (Returns-Refunds)
concepts.
 Provide Business insights.

3) Data Description
Dataset name: E-commerce Dataset.xlsx
Columns Used: Date, Region, Product, Quantity, Unit Price, Store Location, Customer Type,
Salesperson, Total price, Payment Method, Returned, Order ID, Customer ID, Shipping cost, Order
date, Delivery Date, Region Manager, Total Revenue.
Data Source: Sample dataset for analysis.

4) Data Cleaning
 Remove Duplicate orders
 Converted data columns into proper format
 Ensured correct calculation of total sales (Price*Quantity)
 Returned Products should be recorded as negative values in total Revenue, while completed
sales should be recorded as positive values.

5) Analysis & Insights
1. The Business generated 2.18 M revenue (2023-2025) but a 24.80% return rate is high, Since
returns are recorded as negative values, they significantly reduce net revenue and
profitability.

2. In Overall and half year, The North region leads in revenue (0.33M) while West is the lowest
in first half year (0.19M). This Indicates strong performance in some regions but growth
opportunities in weaker areas.

3. Products like Desk, Laptop and Printer generate the highest revenue in E-Commerce dataset
showing that sales are driven by a few high value items.

4. By Half Year Comparison (2023-2025), From Jan -Jun, Revenue increased from 2023 (430K)
to 2024 (458K), followed by a decline in 2025 (428K). This suggest a recent slowdown after a
peak in 2024, If the return product might get increase in remaining half year of 2025, then
the revenue might get low compared to 2023-2024.

5. In half year comparison, The elevate Returned product in 2025 compared to other years,
suggests potential issue in product quality, inaccurate product representation which may be
impacting customer satisfaction.

7) Conclusion
Revenue grew by 12.09% in 2024 vs 2023, but declined in 2025 (H1). This drop is mainly due to
sharp increase in returns, which offset sales and reduced net revenue. If this trend continues, it
may further impact performance.

8) Recommendation for Remaining Months
 Focus marketing on top marketing categories for Remaining months.
 Provide good quality & Less delivery delays.
 Introduce Targeted offers during Peak seasons.
 Expand reach in other region especially West Region.
 Provide Personalized Recommendations to Customers.
